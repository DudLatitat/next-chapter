# Next Chapter · 新篇章

Rudy's private Android app for the road to China: learn Chinese for real life there, and track the move.

- **Today**: countdown to the move and the next Chengdu trip, today's practice, phrase of the day, next move step.
- **Learn**: ~85 starter phrases in situation decks (Yuling, her family, food, paying, transport, paperwork, Sichuanese…), spaced-repetition review in two directions (Read 读 / Say 说), the phone's Chinese voice, your own phrases.
- **Move**: move and trip dates, savings goal with monthly pace, flight price target, checklist.
- **Progress**: streak, known words, 12-week calendar, move-ready %.
- **Extras**: home-screen widget, daily reminder, Listen 听 quiz, tone trainer and tone-coloured pinyin, quick-add from WeChat (select text → *Add to Next Chapter*, auto pinyin), survival phrasebook with big text, backup/restore to a file.

Everything stays on the phone: no account, no server, works in China without a VPN.

Built on the Two Skies stack (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, night-sea theme). Each push builds an APK on GitHub Actions; grab it from the **latest** release.
